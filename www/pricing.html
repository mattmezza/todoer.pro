Good questions. Clear answers.

Do external agents spend Todoer AI credits?

Core task operations through MCP, CLI and REST do not spend Todoer credits. Your external AI client handles its own provider usage. Optional built-in AI actions will require credits or a configured personal key once billing and BYOK ship.

Can I buy credits now?

No. Credit sales, Stripe billing and a spend overview are planned. Pack prices and purchase terms will be published before checkout opens.

Can I bring my own AI key?

Per-account BYOK is planned. You will pay your provider directly, with no Todoer AI usage fee. Today, built-in assistance is configured by the instance operator.

Will Todoer always have the same pricing?

Pricing may change as the service evolves. Published terms will explain any paid offer before purchase.

Self-hosting and everyday use.

Can I self-host Todoer for free?

Yes. You can run Todoer yourself without paying a Todoer subscription. You provide and pay for your infrastructure, storage and optional AI services, and manage updates, security and backups. Follow the repository deployment instructions and release requirements.

What is the difference between hosted and self-hosted?

The planned hosted service runs the app for you. Self-hosting puts deployment and operations in your hands. Free software access does not include a server, managed backups or a hosted-service support commitment.

Do I need technical experience to self-host?

You should be comfortable running a web service, configuring its environment and authentication, and maintaining persistent storage and backups. If you want to avoid operating a server, the hosted option is intended to provide that convenience.

Can I organize both personal and project tasks?

Yes. Use workspaces and lists to separate areas of your life, sections to group work and nested tasks to break larger outcomes into smaller actions. Start with a single list if that is all you need.

Can I share a list with someone else?

Todoer supports workspace roles and list-level access. Review the audience before sharing or moving work between spaces. Sharing should be a deliberate choice, especially when tasks contain private notes or attachments.

Can I use recurring tasks and reminders?

The app supports recurrence and configured reminders. Email and push delivery depend on the services and device permissions available in your deployment; self-hosted operators need to configure those services.

Does Todoer work offline?

You can read cached recent lists and queue captures for synchronization. This is not full offline editing. Reconnect and confirm pending tasks have synchronized before clearing browser storage or changing devices.

Can I export my work?

Export formats include text, CSV, JSON and ZIP. Choose a format suited to the information you need to preserve. For self-hosted recovery, maintain and test database and attachment backups as well.

Is AI required to use Todoer?

No. Capture, lists, task breakdown and planning work without an AI provider. Optional built-in assistance and external AI-agent access are separate ways to add help when you want it.

Can an AI agent change my tasks automatically?

An authorized compatible agent may use the actions its connection permits. Grant access deliberately and review important writes. Built-in AI task-tree drafts have an explicit review-and-apply flow; external clients follow their own confirmation behavior.

How do I contact you privately?

Email info@todoer.pro for product questions, account matters and privacy requests. Never include passwords or access tokens.
