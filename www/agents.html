Three ways to put an agent to work.

01

“Capture this for later.”

Save action items into a list so they are waiting when you return to your day.

02

“What is already on my list?”

03

“Help me break this down.”

Use optional built-in assistance to draft a task tree. Edit it, review it and apply it deliberately.

Connect deliberately. Keep control.

  1. Choose a compatible client. Todoer supports MCP and offers REST and CLI surfaces. Verify the features your client can use.
  2. Authorize the connection. Use the supported account authorization or scoped credentials. Never paste credentials into a public chat.
  3. Start with one small task. Ask for a capture, check where it landed, and build from there.

Claude and ChatGPT are examples of AI environments people may want to use. Their hosted Todoer connector acceptance remains unverified. This is not a claim of an official partnership or one-click integration.
