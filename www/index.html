GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

What is Todoer?

Todoer is a task and project management app for organizing work in spaces, lists, sections, and nested tasks. The rebuild also provides interfaces for AI agents and command-line workflows.

How do AI agents work with Todoer?

The rebuild exposes MCP tools, a REST API, and a CLI. Compatible agents can find and capture tasks and prepare supported changes for review. Availability depends on your client and deployment.

Does an agent need permission to access my tasks?

Agent access uses credentials and is subject to authorization. Available tools and scope depend on the credential and your access to the underlying work.

Is the new Todoer available?

The Rust rebuild is under development. The current app and rebuilt experience may differ; consult the documentation for current capabilities and release status.
