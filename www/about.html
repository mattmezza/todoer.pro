ABOUT TODOER

For everything
you want to do.

Todoer is a place to turn a thought into a task, a task into a plan, and a plan into progress.

Work is changing. The need for clarity isn’t.

We’re rebuilding Todoer around a simple idea: your tools should help you see what needs doing, keep the context that matters, and make it easier to take the next step.

That includes the people you work with and, increasingly, the AI agents you work alongside. Todoer is being built to give both a useful place in your workflow.

The company behind Todoer.

Todoer is created by Merola Software & Services, based at Wartauweg 19, 8049 Zurich, Switzerland.

Built in the open.

Todoer’s source code and rebuild documentation are available on GitHub. Follow along, explore the implementation, and see what’s taking shape.
