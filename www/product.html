MORE WAYS TO WORK

Details that make a difference.

Find your place again

Search across your work and use stable references to connect related tasks and lists.

Keep the conversation nearby

Comments and attachments live with the task, so the reasoning behind a decision is easier to find.

Make a repeatable plan

Plan tasks with dates and priorities, and use recurring tasks for work that comes around again.

Use the keyboard

Move through tasks and capture ideas without breaking your flow. The rebuild also supports command-line workflows.

Share with intention

Organize work in spaces and lists, and review access changes when moving work between them.

Bring your work with you

The rebuild includes account export and import tools. Offline capture is designed to retain pending input on your device and sync later.
