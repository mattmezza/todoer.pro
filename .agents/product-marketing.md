# Product Marketing Context

Draft based on the repository and owner-provided company details; assumptions need validation. Updated 2026-10-27.

## Product overview
Todoer is a task and project management app with browser, API, CLI, and MCP workflows. The Rust rebuild is under development and is not yet a production release. It organizes work in spaces, lists, sections and nested tasks, with planning, discussion and sharing. Optional AI assistance and agent interfaces connect structured work to AI tools. Pricing and business model are unconfirmed.

## Audience and jobs to be done
Working hypothesis: individuals and small teams who organize projects and want both a human interface and programmatic or AI-assisted access. Capture thoughts, break projects into steps, find context, plan work, and coordinate with people or agents. Buyer roles and company segments are unvalidated.

## Problems and differentiation
Work can be scattered across notes, chats and tools; an agent without structured context needs repeated instructions. Todoer combines a task interface with structured access, scoped permissions, CLI and MCP tools, and reviewable supported bulk changes. These are product capabilities, not verified claims of uniqueness or superiority.

## Objections and switching
Users may worry about agent permissions, unintended changes, data retention, and migration. Explain authorization, review and revision checks, and publish accurate deployment and retention details. Availability and connector compatibility must not be overstated. Existing habits and migration effort are plausible barriers, not measured customer feedback.

## Voice and evidence
Direct, calm, specific and technically literate. Avoid inflated productivity promises, invented customer quotes, performance claims and artificial scarcity. Source product statements to docs/rebuild; local tests do not establish production acceptance. No customer testimonials, conversion metrics, competitive research, or verbatim customer interviews have been supplied.
