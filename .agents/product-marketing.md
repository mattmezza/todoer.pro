# Product Marketing Context

Last updated: 2026-10-28. V1 inferred from code and documentation; audience and willingness to pay remain hypotheses for founder review.

## Product Overview
Todoer is open-source, keyboard-friendly task management for capturing commitments, breaking down projects and deciding the next action. Web app, CLI, REST and MCP surfaces. MIT source; hosted business model undecided. Production acceptance remains outstanding.

## Target Audience
Hypothesis: independent makers, developers and solo professionals who want structured task context without enterprise administration. Jobs: capture a thought immediately; turn a project into executable steps; review commitments deliberately. Small-group sharing is supported; enterprise procurement is not the primary pitch.

## Personas
Primary user and buyer are initially the same person. Technical users also evaluate source access, credentials, exports and self-hosting costs.

## Problems & Pain Points
Scattered commitments, vague project tasks and overly elaborate systems. Switching costs include rebuilding lists and learning another workflow. These are inferred problems, not customer interview findings.

## Competitive Landscape
Todoist: broad integrations and mature collaboration; difficult to challenge on ecosystem breadth. TickTick: extensive personal productivity features and calendar tools; Todoer should serve buyers who prefer a narrower task workflow. Things: Apple-focused experience and device-specific purchases; Todoer offers a browser-oriented workflow and open source. These tradeoffs are positioning judgments, not usability test results.

## Differentiation
Combination of nested tasks, deliberate AI review, permission-aware agent access, CLI and inspectable MIT code. No claim that each capability is unique. Focus on useful control rather than maximal feature count.

## Objections
- Is the rebuild ready? Publish the actual release status; final production acceptance is outstanding.
- Will I lose control to AI? Core capture needs no model; drafts require explicit review.
- Why pay if it is open source? Hosted operations and support could offer convenience; pricing and service commitments require definition.
Anti-persona: buyers needing audited enterprise assurances, guaranteed connector compatibility or full offline editing today.

## Switching Dynamics
Push: fragmented capture and cumbersome planning. Pull: a focused workspace with task context and optional automation. Habit: existing lists and shortcuts. Anxiety: migration, longevity, data portability and hosting reliability.

## Customer Language
No verified verbatim customer quotes available. Do not manufacture them. Use: capture, next action, lists, review, your work. Avoid: revolutionary, guaranteed productivity gains, unlimited AI, lifetime hosting.

## Brand Voice
Direct, practical, independent, lightly irreverent. Familiar headline from the legacy site: “Todos without the bullshit.” Body copy stays concrete. New identity follows rebuild slate/blue atmosphere, Inter/system sans and the current t favicon.

## Proof Points
No customer counts, testimonials or quantified productivity benefits supplied. Evidence: repository, current implementation ledger and bounded test documentation. Keep implemented scope distinct from production acceptance.

## Goals
Build qualified interest and app exploration at app.todoer.pro from todoer.pro. Publish useful guides, validate hosted willingness to pay and avoid premature perpetual-service commitments. Metrics and current AI visibility unknown.
